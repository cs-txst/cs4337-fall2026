Label 0 is not an object
Every pixel that failed the threshold gets label
      0, so the first row of stats describes the background and is almost always
      the largest thing in the array. The notebook's range(1, nb_components)
      exists entirely to skip it.

    
Two rules, two answers
Nothing in the image changes when you switch
      between 4 and 8. The pixels are identical; only the question "are these two touching?"
      changes, and with it the number of objects you report. At threshold 1 this page counts
      2,546 components under the 4-rule and 1,341 under the 8-rule from the same mask.

    
Why largest works here
Only because the car is the biggest moving thing in
      frame. Move the camera, add a second car, or let a branch fill the foreground and the
      largest component is no longer the one you wanted. It is an assumption, not a
      method.

    
What the threshold costs
Drag it to 1 and the leaves and sensor noise come
      back as hundreds of components. Drag it to 100 and the largest surviving piece of the
      car is a few dozen pixels. The window where this works is narrower than the lecture's
      single chosen number suggests.
