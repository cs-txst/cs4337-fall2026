Why two differences
A single absdiff is symmetric. It says
      the same thing about frame 30 as it says about frame 41, so the car appears at both
      positions and there is nothing in the image to tell them apart. The second difference
      supplies the missing constraint.

    
Why the minimum
The car's position in frame 30 is bright in both
      differences. Its position in the two neighbours is bright in only one of them.
      cv.min keeps what both agree on, which is exactly the first set. Try
      max and both ghosts come straight back.

    
What the gap buys
A small gap gives a thin, crisp silhouette but very
      little signal. A large gap gives a thicker silhouette and much more background drift.
      The counters above move in opposite directions as you drag it, which is the whole
      tradeoff on slide 10.
