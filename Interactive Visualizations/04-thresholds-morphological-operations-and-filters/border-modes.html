Try this first
Set the kernel to 9 x 9 and switch to
      BORDER_CONSTANT. A dark frame appears around the whole picture, because the
      filter is averaging real pixels with a border of black that was never photographed.

    
Why the default is what it is
BORDER_REFLECT_101 mirrors without
      repeating the edge pixel itself. That keeps the invented values statistically like their
      neighbours and adds no artificial edge for a gradient filter to find.

    
The one that surprises people
BORDER_WRAP pulls in the opposite
      side of the image. It is right for genuinely periodic data and wrong for photographs,
      where it smears the sky into the ground.

    
Why this matters later
Every convolution layer in a CNN makes this same
      choice, usually zero padding, which is BORDER_CONSTANT with value 0. The dark
      frame you can see here is the reason padding is a design decision and not a detail.
