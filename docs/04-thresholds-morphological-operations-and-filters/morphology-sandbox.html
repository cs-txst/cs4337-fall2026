The question from deck slide 13
If erosion and dilation were inverses,
      opening would do nothing at all. Load Speckled blob, predict the result of Open
      before you press it, then play it. The speckles die during erosion and never come
      back.

    
Why they are not inverses
Erosion destroys information. Dilation cannot
      invent back what is no longer there. That asymmetry is the entire reason opening and
      closing are useful rather than redundant.

    
Open versus close
Load Thin bridge. Open snaps the bridge; close
      leaves it. Load Holes. Close fills them; open does not. Open removes what is
      too small to be there. Close fills what is too small to be missing.

    
The shape matters
Run the same erosion with a square and then a cross.
      The cross leaves diagonal corners alone because it never reaches them. This is why
      cv.getStructuringElement takes a shape and not just a size.
