Try this first
Put the church image on THRESH_BINARY and drag
      T slowly from 60 to 200. Ask the class to call out the T where the roofline breaks.
      There is no single right answer, which is the point of deck slide 9.

    
Then this
Switch to THRESH_TRUNC without moving T. The picture
      stays recognisable because dark pixels keep their own values. The transfer function
      panel shows why in one glance.

    
And this
Press Sweep T and watch the marker on the variance curve.
      It falls into the valley between the two humps of the histogram. That valley is all
      Otsu is looking for.

    
Where it breaks
Choose the bird photo and jump to Otsu. The histogram has
      one broad hump, not two, so the cut lands somewhere arbitrary. Otsu assumes two
