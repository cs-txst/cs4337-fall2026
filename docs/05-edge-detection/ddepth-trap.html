Why it happens

A Sobel response is signed. A dark-to-light edge gives a positive number and a light-to-dark edge gives a negative one, and abs is there to fold the two together. But ddepth = -1 means keep the input type, and the input is uint8. Every negative result saturates to 0 on its way out of filter2D, before abs ever runs.

Switch direction
The dy kernel loses an even larger share, because this
      photograph has more horizontal contrast going down than up. The fraction depends on the
      picture, which is why you cannot eyeball it.

    
Why nobody notices
The broken version still produces a plausible edge picture.
      Nothing raises an error, no value goes out of range, and the result is half an edge map
      that looks like a whole one.

    
The scaling switch
Dividing by sum(abs(dx)) is
      cosmetic and cv.Sobel does not do it. Turn it off and the peak
      grows by exactly eight. It changes no decision, but it does change what threshold you need.
