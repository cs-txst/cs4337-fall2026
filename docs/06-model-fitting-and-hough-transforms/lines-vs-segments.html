What maxLineGap does
At threshold 25 with minLineLength 190, a maxLineGap of 2 returns a single segment: almost nothing on this road is 190 pixels of unbroken paint. Raise maxLineGap to 100 and the same votes return 18, because fragments now join across the gaps and clear the length bar. maxLineGap is what makes a dashed line one line.

    
What minLineLength does
It pulls the other way. At maxLineGap 25, dropping minLineLength from 260 to 20 takes the count from 4 to 44, and the 40 extra are short pieces of grass, gravel and shadow. minLineLength is what throws away accidental alignments.

    
Where the segments come from
The frame is from the highway video that ships with the course notebooks, cropped to the road surface and masked to a trapezoid before voting, which is what a real lane pipeline does. Every segment and every line drawn here is genuine OpenCV output, precomputed for all 4 × 6 × 6 parameter combinations these sliders can reach. Only the top 60 infinite lines are drawn, because at the lowest threshold there are 537 of them and they black out the frame.
