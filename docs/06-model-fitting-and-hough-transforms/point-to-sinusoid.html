Watch for this
Place two points and their curves cross exactly once, because two points determine one line. Place a third point off that line and you get three crossings instead of one, and none of them collects all three curves. Only genuine collinearity produces a single cell that every curve passes through.

    
The convention
ρ = x·cos(θ) + y·sin(θ), the convention used by the deck, by cv.HoughLines and by the accompanying notebook. θ is the direction of the perpendicular from the origin to the line, not the direction of the line itself.

    
How the crossing is found
The best crossing is a vote in a 1 degree by 3 pixel grid, the same idea as the accumulator. Three points on x + y = 360 all give ρ = 254.6 at θ = 45°, which lands in the cell labeled 256.
