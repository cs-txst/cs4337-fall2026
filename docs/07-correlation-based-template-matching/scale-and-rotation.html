A peak, not the edge of the range
On the zoomed photo the best score sits near 0.54 between scales 1.3 and 1.8, climbs to 0.797 at 2.9, then falls: 0.794 at 3.0, 0.73 at 3.3. The notebook's range stops at 3.0, so it is worth checking that 2.9 is a real peak, and it is.

    
Small templates win by accident
Upright, the tilted photo's best match is at scale 0.3 on the shirt, 0.616. A 15 by 18 template has so few pixels that plain texture can resemble it. Rotate the template by -12 degrees at scale 1.0 and the real face scores 0.717 (0.716 in the notebook, which pads the image) and wins.

    
What the search costs
28 scales times 21 angles is 588 calls to cv.matchTemplate for one photo, and each extra transformation multiplies it again. Every score here is OpenCV's, computed without padding; the notebook pads the image, which changes scores only near the border.
