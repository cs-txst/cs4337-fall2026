Lighting is not identity
On raw pixels a photo taken in brighter light is far from a darker photo of the same person. Normalizing gives every face mean 0 and standard deviation 1, and the matches improve from 7 to 9 of 10. New photo 0 shows it best: compare raw and normalized.

    
Ten numbers are enough
With 10 eigenface numbers per face the matcher gets as many right as with all 3,000 pixels. With 1 to 3 it fails: the first eigenfaces mostly describe what all faces share. Slide the number down and watch where it breaks.

    
Nearest is not the same as matching
Remove a person and their photo still has a nearest neighbor, a stranger. Turn the threshold on and look at the dot plot: no threshold puts every blue dot on the accepted side and every orange square on the rejected side, so every choice trades rejected owners for accepted strangers.
