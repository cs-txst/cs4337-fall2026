k-NN Playground

Three classes of labeled points and one query, the star. The query gets the class that wins a vote among its K nearest points, and the background shows the class every other spot on the plane would get. Change K and the distance, click to move the query, or add points of your own and watch the boundaries move.

Neighbors

A tie in the vote goes to the tied class whose point is nearest, the rule of the notebook's knn function.

Distance

The outline around the star passes through its K-th neighbor: every point inside is closer. Its shape is the distance's idea of "equally far": a circle for L2, a diamond for L1, a square for L∞.

K = 1 draws every point's own territory
With one neighbor, each spot takes the class of the single closest point, so the plane breaks into cells around the points and a single stray point owns a pocket of the wrong color. Add the stray B and watch the pocket appear at K = 1 and vanish at K = 3 or 5.

    
Large K smooths, then blurs
A larger K lets several neighbors outvote one odd point, and the boundaries straighten. Push K toward the size of the smallest class and the smallest class starts losing even near its own points: with only a few examples per class, a big vote is a vote among strangers.

    
The distance decides who is near
L1 adds the differences along each axis, L∞ keeps only the largest, L2 is the straight-line distance. For images the same choice applies to thousands of pixel differences at once. OpenCV's cv.ml.KNearest uses L2 only.
