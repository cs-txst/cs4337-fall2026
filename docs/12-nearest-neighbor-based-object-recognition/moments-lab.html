Each kind ignores one more thing
Raw moments measure positions from the image corner, so moving the shape changes them. Central moments measure from the shape's own centroid and ignore the move. Normalized central moments also divide out the size. Hu's seven combinations also ignore the turn.

    
Mirroring flips one sign
A mirror image has the same six first Hu moments and the opposite seventh. That is the only change Hu moments notice, and only through I7's sign, which is why the log scale keeps the sign.

    
Ignoring a turn has a price
A 9 in this font is a 6 turned upside down, so their Hu moments agree (to the pixel grid) and no distance on them can separate the two. For digits, orientation is part of the answer.
