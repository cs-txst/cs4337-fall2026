One number decides it
On (w − 3)2 every step multiplies the distance to 3 by 1 − 2η. At η = 0.1 that is 0.8: 0, 0.6, 1.08, 1.46. At 0.5 it is 0, one jump to the answer. At 1.0 it is −1, bouncing between 0 and 6 forever, and at 1.1 it is −1.2: 0, 6.6, −1.32, 8.18, worse every step.

    
The steepest direction sets the limit
On the bowl each direction has its own factor, 1 − η across and 1 − cη up and down. Above η = 2/c the steep direction blows up while the gentle one is still crawling: with c = 10, 0.205 bounces out after 25 steps, while 0.02 has covered only 40% of the way across.

    
Why this matters for networks
A network's loss has millions of directions with very different steepness. The learning rate has to stay below the limit of the steepest one, so the gentle ones are slow, which is why training runs for thousands of steps and why the learning rate is the first setting to tune.
