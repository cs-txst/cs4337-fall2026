0 hidden units is a line
The linear classifier can only cut the plane with straight lines, three of them meeting in the middle, and gets 49% of the points right after the notebook's 200 steps. Training longer barely helps: even the best straight cuts are poor on a spiral.

    
Hidden units bend the boundary
Each ReLU unit adds one fold along a straight line. With 100 of them and 3,000 steps the network reaches 99%, the notebook's number; 10 are already enough for 99% here, and 3 give 62%. These are accuracies on the training points themselves.

    
No ReLU, no network
Turn the activation off and the 100 hidden units collapse into one matrix: two layers become W2 W1, a linear classifier again, and the accuracy falls back to the linear 49%. The ReLU is the whole difference.
