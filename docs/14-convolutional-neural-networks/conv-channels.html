Conv Layer, Channel by Channel

One filter of a convolutional layer on a color CIFAR-10 photo. The filter has one slice per channel; at every position it multiplies each slice with its channel, adds all the products and one bias, and writes one number into the activation map. Pick a filter (Sobel, a color filter, or one that the notebook's network learned), click the output map or press play, and change the stride and the padding.

One filter, one map
However many channels go in, one filter gives one activation map: the three channel sums are added, not kept apart. A layer with six filters gives six maps, and the next layer's filters have six slices.

    
No flip
PyTorch, like cv.filter2D, does not flip the kernel: it computes correlation. Sobel x responds positively where the photo gets brighter to the right, here exactly as in the edge detection lecture.

    
The bias is a threshold
With a ReLU after it, a negative bias decides how strong a match must be before anything comes out. Slide it down with the Sobel filter and watch the weak edges disappear.
