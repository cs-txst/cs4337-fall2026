Six filters, six maps
Each of conv1's filters is drawn as a tiny color picture of its 5 × 5 × 3 weights. Its map is bright where the photo locally looks like that picture. Some are edge detectors of one orientation, some respond to one color.

    
Equivariant, then (roughly) invariant
Move the photo and every conv1 map moves by the same amount. The pooling and fully connected layers turn the maps into one answer, which should not move. It does move, sometimes by a lot for a shift of a few pixels, which is why augmentation trains on shifted photos.

    
Where the parameters are
conv1 and conv2 hold 2,872 of the 62,006 parameters. fc1, which turns the 400 numbers of the last pooled maps into 120, holds 48,120 of them.
