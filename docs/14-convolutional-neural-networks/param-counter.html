Depth comes for free in the filter
A filter always spans every input channel, so its size is F × F × C, not F × F. Forgetting the C is the most common counting mistake: ten 5 × 5 filters on a color image have 10 × (5 × 5 × 3 + 1) = 760 parameters, not 260.

    
The image size does not matter
Change N and the convolution's count stays put: the same filters slide over a bigger image. The fully connected and locally connected counts grow with every output pixel, which is why nobody builds them for photos.

    
When the stride does not fit
If (N − F + 2P) / S + 1 is not a whole number, PyTorch rounds down and quietly ignores the last rows and columns of the input. Try 7 × 7 with a 3 × 3 filter and stride 3.
