Three small layers or one big one
Three 3 × 3 convolutions see 7 × 7, like a single 7 × 7 convolution, with 27C² weights instead of 49C² and three ReLUs instead of one. VGG is built on that trade.

    
Pooling makes it grow fast
Without pooling, ten 3 × 3 layers see 21 pixels. With a 2 × 2 pooling step every few layers the same depth covers the whole photo: in the notebook's network a unit after the second pooling layer sees 16 × 16 of the 32 × 32 image.

    
Bigger than the image
A receptive field can be wider than the input. The extra part falls on the padding, and the unit simply sees all of the image, which is what the last layers of a classifier should.
