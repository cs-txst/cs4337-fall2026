The last block is most of the network
layer4 holds 8.4 million of ResNet-18's 11.2 million parameters (with a 17-class head). "Fine-tune only the last block" still trains three quarters of the weights; what it saves is the backward pass through the early layers and the risk of disturbing them.

    
Backward stops at the first trainable block
Gradients are needed only from the loss down to the earliest trainable parameter. With everything but the head frozen, backpropagation ends at the head, which is why feature extraction is fast, and why the frozen part's output can be computed once and stored.

    
Order matters
The freezing loop freezes every parameter the model has at that moment. Run it after replacing the head and the new head is frozen too: nothing is trainable, and torch.optim refuses an empty parameter list.
