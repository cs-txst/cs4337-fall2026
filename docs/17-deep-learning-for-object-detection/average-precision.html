One threshold, one point
Precision and recall describe the detector at one score threshold. Slide it: recall can only rise as more boxes are kept, and precision mostly falls. AP is the area under all of those points at once, so it needs no threshold.

    
What the misses are
Pick the crowded photos and lower the threshold. Most red boxes sit on real people in the background whom the dataset does not label. Fine-tuning removes most of them: the detector learns this dataset's idea of a pedestrian.

    
Tighter boxes, lower AP
Switch the IoU requirement to 0.9. The same boxes that scored an AP above 0.9 now score about 0.5. Nothing got worse: the boxes are in the right place and not exact, and IoU is strict.
