Non-Max Suppression, Step by Step

The lecture's detector, a pretrained Faster R-CNN, returns 252 "person" boxes for this street photo before any cleanup. Non-max suppression keeps the best box, deletes the boxes that overlap it too much, and repeats. Step through it, then change the IoU threshold and the score threshold and see which duplicates survive.

Greedy, and that is all
NMS never looks at the photo. It sees boxes and scores, keeps the highest score, and deletes by overlap. The first step here removes dozens of near-copies of one pedestrian at once.

    
No threshold is right
Raise the NMS IoU to 0.9 and duplicates survive: several confident boxes on one person. Lower it to 0.2 and nobody is lost here, because these pedestrians barely overlap. In a dense crowd the boxes of neighbors overlap more than that, and a low threshold deletes real people.

    
Two different knobs
NMS removes duplicates of the same object. The score threshold removes guesses. With the score at 0.05 many faint boxes remain in the background; at 0.8 only confident ones are left, including people the dataset did not label.
