No loss to watch
Between epoch 1 and epoch 10 the images turn from blobs into digits, and the generator's loss does not fall: it is higher at the end (2.62) than at the start (1.92). From epoch 4 on, the discriminator gives real digits about 0.86 and generated ones about 0.14, epoch after epoch. Both networks improve together, so the balance between them says little. A GAN is judged by its images.

    
Sharp from the start
Even the poor images of epoch 1 are made of crisp strokes. A blurry average would be easy for the discriminator to spot, so the generator never learns to make one. Compare with the gray smears of the L2 loss.

    
Uneven coverage
The real digits are spread evenly, about 500 of each. The generator's mix changes from epoch to epoch and never becomes even: at the end it draws more than twice as many zeros as the data has, and less than a third as many twos.
